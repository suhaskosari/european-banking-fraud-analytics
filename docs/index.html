Catching fraud across 8 European markets, one transaction at a time.

05GDPR & data governance

The BI layer never sees a direct identifier — pseudonymization runs before export, not after.
  • ✓
    Data minimizationFull name and email are dropped entirely from the analytics layer — never needed for modelling or dashboards.
  • ✓
    PseudonymizationCustomer and account IDs are replaced with a keyed HMAC-SHA256 pseudonym — joinable for analysis, irreversible without the key.
  • ✓
    GeneralizationExact age becomes a 6-band bracket; signup date is generalized to year-month.
  • ✓
    Access controlPostgreSQL role separation — a read-only analyst role has zero grants on the schema holding raw identifiers.
  • ✓
    Human-in-the-loopThe model outputs a risk score and flag, never an automated decision — high-risk transactions route to a fraud analyst.
